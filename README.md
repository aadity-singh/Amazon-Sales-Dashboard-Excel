<h2>📌 Business Problem</h2>

<p>
E-commerce businesses often struggle to track performance across products, regions, and order status, leading to missed opportunities and operational inefficiencies.
</p>

<h2>✅ Solution</h2>

<p>
This dashboard provides a centralized, interactive view of sales data, enabling businesses to monitor KPIs, analyze trends, and make data-driven decisions.
</p>

---

<h2>🔍 Key Business Insights</h2>

<ul>
  <li>⚠️ Only <b>~25% of orders are completed</b>, while ~75% are returned, cancelled, or pending</li>
  <li>📉 Bluetooth Speaker declined <b>~44% YoY</b>, indicating potential saturation</li>
  <li>📈 Echo Dot (<b>+101%</b>) and Wireless Mouse (<b>+67%</b>) show strong growth potential</li>
  <li>🌎 South America leads with <b>~₹7.0M revenue</b> and <b>~₹1.9M profit</b></li>
  <li>💰 Profit margin remains stable at <b>~27%</b></li>
</ul>

---

<h2>🎯 Business Impact</h2>

<ul>
  <li>Identify and scale high-performing products</li>
  <li>Reduce dependency on declining SKUs</li>
  <li>Improve order fulfillment and reduce returns</li>
  <li>Focus on high-performing regions</li>
  <li>Enable faster, data-driven decision-making</li>
</ul>
