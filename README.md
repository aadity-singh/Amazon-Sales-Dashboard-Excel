# 📊 Amazon Sales Dashboard — Excel (2019–2024)

![Dashboard Preview](Amazon_Sales_Dashboard_KPIs_Charts.png)

## 📌 Project Overview
An interactive sales dashboard built in Microsoft Excel analyzing 
₹32M in Amazon sales transactions across 2019–2024. 
The dashboard uncovers critical business insights including a 
75% order failure rate, high-growth products, and regional performance.

---

## 🔍 Key Business Findings
- ⚠️ Only 25% of 5,000 orders completed — 27% returned, 24% cancelled
- 📉 Bluetooth Speaker declined ▼44.2% YoY (₹4.52L → ₹2.44L)
- 📈 Echo Dot grew ▲101.4% & Wireless Mouse ▲67.0% YoY
- 🌎 South America leads with ₹7.0M revenue & ₹1.9M profit
- 💰 Stable 27% profit margin across all 5 regions 2019–2024

---

## 📊 Dashboard Features
- 8 KPI Cards including Completion Rate & Return Rate (warning indicators)
- Sales Trend — Area chart (2019–2024 monthly)
- Profit Trend — Bar chart (2019–2024 monthly)
- Order Status Distribution — Donut chart with callout
- Top 5 Regions by Revenue — Highlighted bar chart
- Product Categories by Profit — Color-coded Treemap
- Top Products & Growth — Table with YoY indicators
- Interactive Slicers — Year, Region, Category, Order Status
- Reset Filters button
- Key Business Insights — Color coded with subheadings

---

## 💡 Business Storytelling Elements
- COVID 2020 annotation on Sales Trend chart
